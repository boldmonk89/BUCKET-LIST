Inflation: 2050 mein prices kitni badhengi?

Inflation matlab mehngai. Aaj jo trip ₹1 lakh ka hai, wo 2050 mein zyada mehnga hoga. Neeche slider hilao, upar ke saare prices turant badal jayenge.

Kam: 2% Zyada: 10%
3.5% saalana mehngai → prices ×4.0 ho jayengi (25 saal mein)

Foreign trips mein dollar/euro ki mehngai bhi lagti hai, isliye unke liye thoda zyada rate (5-6%) realistic hai.
